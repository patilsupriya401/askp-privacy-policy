Privacy Policy

Arena Stick Kings Pro (ASKP)
Effective date: 16 November 2026
Developer: Arush Tech

This Privacy Policy explains how Arush Tech (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use Arena Stick Kings Pro (“ASKP” or the “App”). Please read it carefully.

1. Gameplay and account options

ASPK includes offline Player vs AI gameplay that can be played without signing in. Where account-based features are enabled, the App may offer Google Sign-In or Guest sign-in. Guest sign-in uses Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous user identity; it is not the same as playing offline without an account.

Online or multiplayer features may be unavailable or disabled in a particular release. This Policy applies to account and online features only when they are made available and used.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, the App may process:

  • Account and authentication information: a Firebase user ID, authentication provider, and account status. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide your name, email address, and profile photo, subject to the permissions and information available from your Google Account.
  • Game profile information: display name, profile photo URL (if available), selected character, wins, losses, and profile creation time.
  • Online gameplay information: information needed to operate online rooms or multiplayer sessions, if those features are enabled and used.
  • Local preferences: game settings such as music, sound effects, and vibration preferences may be stored on your device.

We do not ask you to provide payment-card details to play the offline Player vs AI mode.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to provide sign-in, maintain player profiles, remember selected characters and game statistics, support online gameplay when available, maintain the App, and troubleshoot technical or security issues.

4. Service providers

ASPK uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, for account authentication and cloud profile/game data when account-based features are used. These services process information on our behalf to provide their functionality.

Google’s privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy. Firebase information is available at Firebase Privacy and Security.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described in this Policy, or disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the App, its users, or our rights.

6. Data storage and security

Cloud account and profile information is stored using Firebase services. Local game preferences are stored on your device. We rely on the security features provided by these services and take reasonable steps to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data retention and account deletion requests

We retain account and profile information while it is needed to provide the relevant App features. You may request deletion of your ASKP account and associated data by emailing askp.support@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Google sign-in, if applicable. For a Guest account, include the Firebase user ID if you can access it and explain that the account is a Guest account. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request.

After verifying a valid request, we will take steps to delete the associated account and personal data under our control, subject to information we are legally required or permitted to retain. Some data may remain temporarily in secure backups before being overwritten in the ordinary backup cycle.

Important: this email contact is an external request channel. The App must also provide a readily discoverable in-app path to initiate account deletion if account creation is available. The developer must implement and test that in-app path and the corresponding deletion process before publishing the account-creation feature on Google Play.

8. Children’s privacy

ASPK is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an account-based feature, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the App or its data practices change. The updated version will be posted at the same public policy URL with a revised effective date.

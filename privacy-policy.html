Privacy Policy

ASKP (Arena Stick Kings Pro)
Effective date: 17 November 2026
Developer: Arush Tech

This Privacy Policy explains how Arush Tech ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use ASKP ("the App").

1. Offline Gameplay

ASKP is an offline game. The current version provides offline gameplay against AI and does not require an internet connection, account registration, login, or sign-in to play.

The current version does not provide online multiplayer, cloud-based player profiles, or online account services.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of ASKP does not require users to provide personal information. We do not collect or transmit personal information through the game's offline gameplay functionality.

Game preferences, such as sound, music, and other gameplay settings, may be stored locally on your device so that your preferences can be maintained.

These local settings are not transmitted to our servers by the current version of the App.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored game preferences are used only to maintain your gameplay settings and improve your experience when using the App.

The current version does not use personal information for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The current version of ASKP does not integrate advertising services, third-party analytics services, or account authentication services.

We may consider introducing advertising services, such as Google AdMob, in a future version. If advertising or other third-party services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the applicable data collection, processing, and sharing practices before those features are made available.

5. Sharing of Information

The current version of ASKP does not collect or transmit personal information for sharing with third parties.

We do not sell users' personal information.

6. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay preferences may be stored locally on your device. The current version does not maintain cloud-based user accounts or store player profile information on our servers.

You can clear locally stored App data or uninstall the App through your device settings. Doing so may remove locally stored game preferences.

7. Children's Privacy

The current version of ASKP does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through its offline gameplay functionality.

If you have questions about children's privacy in relation to the App, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
